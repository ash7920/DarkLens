        How to label (read this first)
        
        
          
        Ask one question: is this element trying to push the shopper into something they might not
            choose if it were presented neutrally? Judge the element shown, using the screenshot only for
            context. When in doubt, it is not a dark pattern.
        
          
        Most items are ordinary shop content and get 0 Not a dark pattern:
            product names, prices, sizes, weights, colours, ratings counts like "(31)", menu links ("Home",
            "Facial care"), plain discounts ("-14%"), delivery costs, legal or error text.
        
          
        
            
        1 Urgency
        
            
        A deadline or timer that pressures a quick decision. "Offer ends in 02:14:55", "Order in the next 3 hours
              for delivery tomorrow", "Today only". Not: a plain date or time, a price, a sale with no deadline.
        
            
        2 Scarcity
        
            
        Claims that stock or availability is limited. "Only 2 left", "Only a few left in stock", "Almost sold out",
              "High demand". Not: "In stock", "Available online".
        
            
        3 Social proof
        
            
        Pressure from what other people are doing. "13 people viewed this item in the past day", "Bought 500 times
              this week", "4% bought", "Most booked hotels". Not: a star rating or review count on its own.
        
            
        4 Confirmshaming
        
            
        The "no" option makes you feel guilty or stupid. "No thanks, I don't like saving money".
        
            
        5 Pre-ticked add-ons / sneaking
        
            
        Something extra is already selected or added for you: a box marked [checkbox, PRE-TICKED] for insurance,
              a newsletter or a donation; an add-on or fee slipped into the basket. Not: a size or delivery
              option you have to choose anyway.
        
            
        6 Difficult cancellation
        
            
        Leaving, cancelling or unsubscribing is made hard: "call us to cancel", long detours, hidden cancel links.
        
            
        7 Interface interference
        
            
        The layout steers your choice. Lines like "'Accept all' (large) vs 'Reject' (small)" mean the option good
              for the shop is much bigger or brighter than its alternative in the same choice (cookies, consent,
              add-on yes/no). Not: two unrelated links (e.g. "Home" vs "Cart") or a privacy-policy link.
        
            
        8 Forced action
        
            
        You must do something unrelated to continue: create an account to check out, accept marketing to see
              prices, install an app to buy.
        
          
        
          
        Take your time: 5 to 15 seconds per item is normal. Use S to skip if you need to come back.
